## 🐛 KNOWN LIMITATIONS

### **Data Quality**:
1. **Sparse luas_ha**: Only 10 cases have area data
2. **Unspecified provinces**: 8 conflicts marked "Sulawesi (unspecified)"
3. **Multiple companies per case**: Some cases involve multiple companies (split by semicolon)
4. **No exact coordinates**: Can't do precise geospatial mapping yet

### **Visualization Constraints**:
1. **Long company names**: May truncate in table cells
2. **Multiple problem types**: Split by semicolon, may look cluttered
3. **No direct IUP cross-reference**: Can't yet match conflicts to official permit database

### **User Experience**:
1. **Heavy text**: Hero narrative is long (users may skim)
2. **Multiple filters**: Requires understanding of data structure
3. **External links**: Some sources may be paywalled or moved
